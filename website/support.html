2. TestFlight Beta Installation Guide

  1. Download and install Apple TestFlight from the iOS App Store on your iPhone.
  2. Click the TestFlight Beta Link or submit your Apple ID email on our homepage to receive an invite.
  3. Accept the invitation in TestFlight and tap Install.
  4. Open Rooka, create your account, configure your athlete profile, and connect your favorite tracking devices.

3. Wearables & Integrations Troubleshooting

How do I push workouts to Apple Watch via WorkoutKit?

In Rooka, navigate to your planned workout for today and tap "Push to Watch". Rooka uses Apple's native WorkoutKit framework to export your warmup, structured interval steps, target heart rate or power zones, and cooldown directly into the native Apple Workout app on your Apple Watch. When you're ready to train, open the Workout app on your watch, tap the custom Rooka workout, and receive live target guidance and haptic interval beeps.

How do I push workouts to Garmin Connect Calendar?

In Rooka, go to Settings > Integrations > Garmin Connect to link your account. Once authorized, tap "Push to Garmin" on any scheduled workout. Rooka synchronizes the structured steps and targets straight onto your Garmin Connect Calendar, automatically transferring them to your compatible Garmin watch or Edge cycling computer upon your next sync.

How do I connect Strava?

In Rooka, go to Settings > Integrations > Strava. Tap "Authorize with Strava" and approve read/write permissions. Once connected, every ride, run, or swim is pulled instantly upon completion.

4. Account Management & Data Deletion

You have the right to export or completely delete your account at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This permanently removes your credentials, workouts, recovery logs, and telemetry history.
  • Email Deletion Request: Send an email from your account address to privacy@rooka.io with the subject line "Delete My Account". All associated data will be removed within 30 days.
