Privacy Policy for Rooka

Effective Date: August 18, 2026  |  Domain: rooka.io

Rooka ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Rooka mobile application (the "App") and the website located at https://rooka.io. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal data, health metrics, and workout logs when you use our services.

Please read this policy carefully. By downloading, accessing, or using Rooka, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is capable of being associated with you ("Personal Data").

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Name, username, email address, password hash, and profile preferences.
  • Physical & Health Metrics: Age, biological sex, body weight, height, resting heart rate, and training targets.
  • AI Coach Interactions: Text queries, feedback, voice notes, and training context shared with the Rooka AI Coach.

B. Information Synchronized from Connected Wearables & Platforms

With your explicit authorization, Rooka connects to third-party fitness APIs (Strava, Garmin Connect, and Apple HealthKit) to import:

  • Workout Activities: Running, cycling, swimming, strength, and multisport activities (distance, moving time, pace, elevation gain, splits, cadence).
  • Biometric Telemetry: Heart rate streams, power output (Watts), HRV (Heart Rate Variability), and estimated training stress score (TSS).
  • OAuth Tokens: Securely stored API access tokens used exclusively to fetch new workouts automatically.

C. Automatically Collected Diagnostic Information

  • Device & App Telemetry: Operating system version, device model, app version, crash diagnostics, and anonymous session performance logs used strictly for debugging and stability improvements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your Personal Data solely to deliver and optimize the core features of Rooka, specifically to:

  1. Calculate Daily Readiness: Compute your individualized Rooka Recovery Score, Acute Training Load (ATL), Chronic Training Load (CTL), and Training Stress Balance (TSB).
  2. Deliver Intelligent AI Coaching: Formulate context-aware workout suggestions, periodization advice, and nutrition guidance tailored to your current fatigue levels.
  3. Synchronize Third-Party Activities: Seamlessly ingest and analyze workout files from Strava, Garmin, and Apple Health.
  4. Biomechanical Load Modeling: Calculate 7-day muscular workload across key muscle groups to balance endurance with functional strength and prevent injuries.
  5. Gamification & Leaderboards: Compute earned Rooka XP and display opt-in leaderboard rankings if public profile features are enabled.

3. Artificial Intelligence & Data Confidentiality

Rooka uses the Google Gemini API to power conversational coaching. We maintain strict privacy and confidentiality standards:

  • Zero Foundation Model Training: Your chat messages, personal metrics, and queries sent to the AI Coach are never used by Google to train public generative AI foundation models without explicit user consent.
  • Data Minimization: Only relevant contextual data (e.g. recent workout intensity, recovery score, and current question) is submitted to generate the coaching response.

4. Health & Medical Disclaimer

⚠️ Rooka is Not a Medical Device or Healthcare Provider.

The training advice, recovery readiness scores, fatigue calculations, and AI coach outputs provided by Rooka are intended solely for general fitness, athletic tracking, and educational purposes. They do not constitute medical advice, clinical diagnoses, or medical treatment plans. Always consult a physician or certified medical professional before embarking on any intense exercise routine or dietary protocol.

5. How We Share & Disclose Information

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or health data. We disclose data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Authorized Third Parties: When you connect Strava, Garmin, or Apple Health, data is exchanged strictly per their developer agreements and your authorization.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Secure database hosting and AI inference endpoints operated under enterprise confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or lawful governmental request.

6. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. All sensitive API tokens are encrypted with AES-256 encryption. All network communication between the Rooka App and our servers occurs exclusively over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

7. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated metrics and workout history at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Rooka, navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account and confirm.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request: Send an email from your registered address to privacy@rooka.io or rutgervandenberg@live.nl with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Upon receiving your request, all personal data is permanently wiped from our active databases within 30 days.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Request access to and a portable copy of your personal data.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate personal metrics.
  • Request erasure (the right to be forgotten).
  • Revoke consent for third-party integrations (Strava/Garmin) at any time in Settings.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us:
