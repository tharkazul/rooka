Exercise Physiology

Data-Driven Periodization

No more guessing whether you're overtraining or under-recovering. Rooka calculates your physiological metrics automatically.

CTL

Chronic Training Load (Fitness)

A rolling 42-day exponentially weighted moving average of your training stress. As your CTL rises steadily without sharp spikes, your endurance base solidifies.

ATL

Acute Training Load (Fatigue)

A short-term 7-day weighted average representing fatigue. Rooka monitors ATL spikes to alert you before injury or overreaching sets in.

TSB

Training Stress Balance (Form)

Fitness minus Fatigue (CTL - ATL). Target positive values (+10 to +25) on race morning to arrive fresh, explosive, and fully recovered.
